# SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM

<div align="center">

# 🌍 The Problem

### *Africa faces five critical barriers to Bitcoin adoption*

</div>

<br />

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" align="center">

### 🔒 Limited Access
Many Africans cannot access Bitcoin because traditional platforms require bank accounts, complicated verification, or technical knowledge.

**60% of adults in Sub-Saharan Africa are unbanked**

</td>
<td width="33%" align="center">

### 🏦 Financial Exclusion
Millions rely on mobile money rather than traditional banks. No bank account = no Bitcoin access.

**Only 23% of adults have a bank account in Africa**

</td>
<td width="33%" align="center">

### 💸 Expensive Transfers
Sending money across borders is slow and costly. Traditional remittance fees average 8-9%.

**Africans pay $4B+ annually in remittance fees**

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="33%" align="center">

### 💳 Can't Spend Bitcoin
Owning Bitcoin isn't the same as using it. No way to spend at local shops or online.

**95% of transactions are still cash-based in Africa**

</td>
<td width="33%" align="center">

### ⚙️ Too Complex
Bitcoin and Lightning are confusing for ordinary users. Technical barriers prevent adoption.

**Only 3% of Africans have ever used cryptocurrency**

</td>
<td width="33%" align="center">

### 🎯 The Gap
The connection between mobile money and Bitcoin doesn't exist yet. This is our opportunity.

**$50B+ mobile money market with no Bitcoin bridge**

</td>
</tr>
</table>
